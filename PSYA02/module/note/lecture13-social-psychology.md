## 一、社会心理学是什么 / What Is Social Psychology

社会心理学研究**成为社会性存在的原因与后果**。  
Social Psychology is the study of the **causes and consequences of being social**.

人类是地球上最具社会性的物种之一（幻灯写为 plant，应为 planet）。  
Humans are among the most social species on the planet.

人类被认为是**超社会（ultrasocial）**的，与蜜蜂等物种类似。  
Humans are considered **ultrasocial** (among other species such as bees).

超社会生物会形成大型社会、**分工（divide labour）**，并为互利而合作。  
Ultrasocial organisms form large societies, **divide labour**, and cooperate for mutual benefit.

## 二、解释他人行为 / Interpreting the Behaviour of Others

本部分核心问题：我们如何解释别人为什么那样做？  
Core question: How do we explain why other people behave the way they do?

---

### 2.1 归因理论 / Attribution Theory

归因：解释**某行为由什么引起**。  
Attribution: explaining **what causes certain behaviour**.

我们可将他人行为归因到两类原因：  
We can attribute others’ behaviour to either:

| 类型 Type | 含义 Meaning | 结论 Conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal** | 来自观察对象内部，如人格 personality | **Dispositional Attribution**（性格归因） |
| **External** | 来自观察对象外部，如情境 situation | **Situational Attribution**（情境归因） |

**内部/性格归因（Internal / Dispositional）**：特质、人格等内部原因。  
**Internal/Dispositional**: trait-based causes inside the person.

**外部/情境归因（External / Situational）**：环境或情境原因。  
**External/Situational**: environmental or situational causes.

一般倾向：把**他人行为**归于内部原因，把**自己行为**归于外部原因。  
Tendency: attribute **others’** behaviour to internal causes, and **our own** to external causes.

#### 例子 / Example

行为：Jane 上班迟到。  
Behaviour: Jane is late for work.

- 内部/性格归因：Jane 不负责任。  
  Internal/Dispositional: Jane is irresponsible.
- 外部/情境归因：Jane 堵车了。  
  External/Situational: Jane is stuck in traffic.

---

### 2.2 Kelley 的共变模型 / Covariation Model of Attributions

Kelley 提出：做内外归因时要考虑三个因素。  
Kelley identified three factors to consider when making internal or external attributions:

1. **一致性（Consistency）**：此人在特定情境中，跨时间行为是否相似？  
   **Consistency**: Is the person’s behaviour similar in particular contexts across time?

2. **独特性（Distinctiveness）**：此人在不同情境中行为是否相似？  
   **Distinctiveness**: Does the person behave similarly across different situations/contexts?

3. **共识（Consensus）**：该行为与其他人的行为是相似还是不同？  
   **Consensus**: Is the behaviour similar or different from other people’s behaviour?

---

### 2.3 行动者–观察者偏差 / Actor–Observer Bias

对自己行为：更倾向用**情境/外部**解释。  
Our behaviour = situational / external causes.

对他人行为：更倾向用**性格/内部**解释。  
Others’ behaviour = dispositional / internal causes.

例子：自己迟到 → 想到车祸/堵车；别人迟到 → 觉得对方懒惰爱睡。  
Example: When *we* are late, we think of traffic; when *others* are late, we think they are lazy.

---

### 2.4 自我服务偏差 / Self-Serving Bias

我们的**成功** → 归内部原因（揽功）。  
Our **successes** = internal causes (take the credit).

我们的**失败** → 归外部原因（甩锅）。  
Our **failures** = external causes (blame outside factors).

例子：考 A+ →「我很聪明」；考 F →「老师不喜欢我」。  
Example: A+ → “I’m so smart”; F → “The teacher doesn’t like me.”

---

### 2.5 基本归因错误 / Fundamental Attribution Error (FAE)

把他人行为过多归因于**内部/性格**，而忽视**外部/环境**因素。  
Attribute others’ behaviour to **internal/dispositional** causes rather than **external/environmental** causes.

例子：别人说「抱歉迟到，堵车了」，观察者却骂「你这人有问题」。  
Example: Someone says “Sorry I’m late, there was traffic,” but observers blame their character.

---

### 2.6 虚假共识效应 / False Consensus Effect

以为「大家都」同意我们的看法，但他们很可能并不同意。  
Thinking that “everyone” agrees with our opinions when they likely do not.

---

### 2.7 印象形成 / Impression Formation

**印象形成**：我们如何对他人形成看法。  
**Impression Formation**: how we create opinions about people.

**第一印象**：最初了解到的关于某人的信息。  
**First Impression**: initial information learned about someone.

第一印象形成很快（秒到分钟），且较持久。  
Made quickly (seconds to minutes) and is enduring.

与**首因效应（Primacy Effect）**相关：先获得的信息影响更大。  
Related to the **Primacy Effect**: early information carries more weight.

**确认偏误（Confirmation Bias）**：更关注并加工与初始印象一致的信息。  
**Confirmation Bias**: we are more likely to attend to and process information consistent with our initial impression.

确认偏误流程：  
Confirmation Bias process:

- 信息与信念一致？→ **是** → 关注支持信念的信息。  
  Consistent with beliefs? → **YES** → attend to supporting information.
- → **否** → 信息被丢弃或忽略。  
  → **NO** → information is discarded or ignored.

---

### 2.8 自我实现预言 / Self-Fulfilling Prophecy

自我实现预言是一个会自我强化的循环。  
A self-fulfilling prophecy is a self-reinforcing cycle.

幻灯流程图（循环）：  
Slide flowchart (cycle):

1. **你对他人的行动**（Your actions toward others）  
2. → **Impact（影响）** → **他人对你的信念**（Others’ beliefs about you）  
3. → **Cause（导致）** → **他人对你的行动**（Others’ actions toward you）  
4. → **Reinforce（强化）** → **你对自己的信念**（Your beliefs about yourself）  
5. → **Influence（再影响）** → 回到你的行动（loop back）

核心：信念与行动相互塑造，最终「预言」成真。  
Core idea: beliefs and actions shape each other until the “prophecy” comes true.

## 三、在他人面前如何行动 / Behaving in the Presence of Others

本部分核心问题：别人在场时，我们的想法和行为如何改变？  
Core question: How do our thoughts and behaviours change when others are present?

---

### 3.1 社会规范与社会影响 / Social Norms & Social Influence

**社会规范（Social Norms）**：规定应有的行为，随情境、文化、时代变化。  
**Social Norms**: prescribed behaviours that vary across context, culture, and time.

**社会影响（Social Influence）**：我们的想法和行为受他人行为影响。  
**Social Influence**: our thoughts and behaviours are affected by the behaviour of others.

**规范性影响（Normative Influence）**常导致我们**从众（conform）**。  
**Normative influence** often causes us to **conform**.

从众：仅仅因为别人在做，自己也跟着做。  
Conformity: simply doing what someone else is doing because they are doing it.

---

### 3.2 Asch 从众实验 / Asch’s Conformity Study

**Solomon Asch** 在 1950 年代研究从众。  
**Solomon Asch** studied conformity in the 1950s.

#### 方法 / Method

被试完成简单知觉任务：比较**一条标准线**与**三条比较线**。  
Participants completed a simple perception task: compare **one standard line** with **3 comparison lines**.

比较时有**托儿（confederates）**在场（3 人及以上）。  
Confederates were present when comparisons were made (3 or more people).

被试要报告哪条比较线与标准线等长。  
Subjects reported which comparison line matched the standard line.

#### 结果 / Findings

约 **75%** 的人至少有一次服从了**明显错误**的答案。  
About **75%** conformed to an obviously incorrect answer at least once.

课堂也提出：这些发现今天是否仍然适用？  
Class also asks: Do these findings still apply today?

---

### 3.3 群体思维 / Groupthink

**群体思维**：当高度从众与群体凝聚力被过度重视时，会出现**有缺陷的决策**（排斥反对信息与观点）。  
**Groupthink**: faulty decision-making when high conformity and group cohesion are highly valued (excluding opposing information and ideas).

#### 群体思维如何运作 / How Groupthink Works

1. **高估群体**（Overestimating the Group）  
2. **封闭心态**（Close-Mindedness）  
3. **要求一致的压力**（Pressure for Uniformity）

#### 历史例子 / Historical Examples

- 挑战者号航天飞机爆炸（Challenger Space Shuttle）  
- 珍珠港（Pearl Harbor）

漫画例子：大家嘴上齐声说「YES」，心里却在想「绝对不行」。  
Cartoon example: people shout “YES!” aloud while thinking “Hell no / This won’t work.”

---

### 3.4 群体认知的其他风险 / Other Risks in Group Cognition

群体决策有其特有风险：  
Groups have their own risks to decision-making:

| 概念 Concept | 定义 Definition |
|---|---|
| **Groupthink** | 群体有时过快达成共识 / Groups sometimes reach consensus too easily |
| **Common Knowledge Effect** | 讨论常围绕人人都知道的信息 / Discussions revolve around information everyone shares |
| **Group Polarization** | 群体决策可能比任何单独成员更极端 / Groups may decide more extremely than any member alone |
| **Diffusion of Responsibility** | 周围人都这样做时，个人感到责任变小 / Individuals feel less responsibility when others act the same way |

---

### 3.5 旁观者效应及相关机制 / Bystander Effect & Related Mechanisms

标题含义：总觉得「别人会帮忙」。  
Theme: thinking someone else will help.

#### 三大概念 / Three Key Concepts

**旁观者效应（Bystander Effect）**：帮助行为与目击者数量**负相关**。  
**Bystander Effect**: helping is negatively correlated with the number of witnesses.

人越多，单个个体出手帮忙的可能性越低。  
The more people present, the less likely any one individual is to help.

**责任分散（Diffusion of Responsibility）**：责任摊到所有在场者身上，结果无人负责。  
**Diffusion of Responsibility**: responsibility is spread over all present, so no one is held accountable.

典型想法：「我以为你会去做！」（I thought you’d do it!）  
Typical thought: “I thought you’d do it!”

**多元无知（Pluralistic Ignorance）**：通过观察他人不行动，错误断定「没事」，于是自己也不行动。  
**Pluralistic Ignorance**: looking at others’ inaction and wrongly concluding nothing is wrong—so you also don’t help.

> 注：幻灯把 pluralistic ignorance 列为学习目标与标题词，但未单独写完整定义；上面按课堂常用含义补全，便于应试。  
> Note: The slides list pluralistic ignorance as a learning objective but do not fully define it; the definition above matches standard course usage for exams.

#### 影响助人行为的三大主题 / Three Themes Affecting Helping

1. **危险程度**（The level of danger present）  
2. **施害者是否在场**（Whether the perpetrator was present or absent）  
3. **所需帮助的类型**（The type of help required）

---

### 3.6 服从权威 / Obedience to Authority

#### 背景 / Background

**Stanley Milgram** 在 1960 年代研究服从权威。  
**Stanley Milgram** studied obedience to authority in the 1960s.

研究背景是对大屠杀暴行的反思。  
Conducted in response to the atrocities of the Holocaust.

#### 研究问题 / Research Question

「正常」人会不会在权威要求下伤害他人？  
Will “normal” people harm others at the request of an authority figure?

#### 实验设置 / The Milgram Experiment

被试被招募参加所谓的「**记忆研究**」。  
Participants were recruited for what they were told was a **“study of memory.”**

电击发生器：30 个开关，每次增加 **15 伏**。  
Shock generator: 30 toggle switches in **15-volt** increments.

角色分配：  
Roles:

- **Participant = Teacher（教师）**  
- **Confederate = Learner（学习者）**（托儿，有脚本化反应）  
- **E = Experimenter（实验者）**

布局：实验者与教师同室；学习者在另一房间；导线连接电击装置与学习者。  
Set-up: Experimenter and Teacher in one room; Learner in a separate room; wire connects shock panel to learner.

#### 主要发现 / Findings

多数被试会在服从权威下继续电击至最高电压。  
Most participants continued to shock the learner until the highest voltage in obedience to an authority figure.

饼图关键数据（幻灯）：约 **65%** 的人打到最高 **450V（XXX）**。  
Pie-chart key finding: about **65%** went to the maximum **450V (“XXX”)**.

其他大致停止点分布（幻灯）：  
Other approximate stopping points from the slide:

- 12.5% → 300V（Intense Shock）  
- 10% → 315V  
- 5% → 330V  
- 各约 2.5% → 345V / 360V / 375V 等

#### 复现研究操纵的因素 / Replication Study Factors

影响服从水平的常见因素包括：  
Factors that affect obedience include:

1. **地点变化**（Varied location）——如名校实验室 vs 普通办公室  
2. **教师与学习者的距离**（Distance between teacher and learner）——越近通常越难继续电击

#### 对被试的影响 / Impact on Participants（Nicolson, 2011）

- 心理痛苦 / 抑郁（Psychological distress / depression）  
- 自我形象改变（Altered self-image）  
- 睡眠紊乱（Disturbed sleep）  
- 创伤（Traumatized participants）

### 3.7 社会角色与情境力量 / Social Roles and the Power of the Situation

#### 斯坦福监狱实验 / Stanford Prison Experiment

**Philip Zimbardo** 在 1970 年代用斯坦福监狱实验考察**情境与社会角色**的力量。  
**Philip Zimbardo** examined the power of situations and social roles in the **Stanford Prison Experiment** (1970s).

（幻灯学习目标拼写为 Standford，正确为 Stanford。）  
(Learning objectives misspell it as “Standford”; correct spelling is Stanford.)

#### 方法要点 / Method

招募年轻男性参加实验。  
Young men were recruited to participate.

招募时所有被试均经筛查，**心理上健康**。  
All participants were screened as **psychologically healthy** at recruitment.

**随机分配**为囚犯（prisoner）或狱警（guard）。  
**Randomly assigned** to be a prisoner or a guard.

#### 过程与结果 / Process & Outcome

第二天起，无力感、去人性化、去个性化开始加剧。  
By day 2, feelings of **powerlessness, dehumanization, and depersonalization** began to escalate.

原计划 **14 天**，因情况恶劣在第 **6 天**提前终止。  
Originally scheduled for **14 days**, but ended after **6 days** due to horrific circumstances.

#### 结论 / Takeaways

说明：**情境与社会角色**对行为影响巨大。  
Illustrates the power of the **situation** and **social roles** on behaviour.

同时也体现了**不伦理的研究实践**。  
Also illustrates **unethical scientific practices**.

## 四、研究伦理 / Ethical Considerations for Research

**贝尔蒙特报告（Belmont Report）**提出人类被试研究的基本伦理原则与指南。  
The **Belmont Report** identified basic ethical principles and guidelines for research with human participants.

### 三大原则 / Three Principles

1. **尊重人（Respect for Persons）** 与 **知情同意（Informed Consent）**  
   Acknowledge autonomy; protect those with diminished autonomy; obtain informed consent.

2. **行善（Beneficence）**：**Do no harm**  
   Maximize possible benefits and minimize possible harms.

3. **公正（Justice）**：不得剥削被试（Subjects should not be exploited）  
   Fair distribution of research benefits and burdens.

> Milgram 与 Stanford Prison Experiment 常被用来对照说明：为何需要这些伦理原则。  
> Milgram and the Stanford Prison Experiment are often used to show why these ethical principles matter.

### 高频易混对比

1. **FAE vs Actor–Observer Bias**  
   FAE 强调「对别人过度性格归因」；Actor–Observer 强调「自己用情境、别人用性格」的双向对比。  
   FAE focuses on over-dispositional judgments of *others*; Actor–Observer contrasts *self vs others*.

2. **Self-Serving Bias vs Actor–Observer Bias**  
   Self-Serving 专看成败：成内败外；Actor–Observer 看自我/他人解释差异。  
   Self-Serving is about success/failure credit; Actor–Observer is self-vs-other explanations.

3. **Diffusion of Responsibility vs Pluralistic Ignorance**  
   前者是「责任被摊开所以不行动」；后者是「误读他人不行动 = 没事」。  
   Diffusion = responsibility diluted; Pluralistic ignorance = misreading inaction as “all clear.”

4. **Conformity vs Obedience**  
   从众是跟同伴/多数；服从是听权威指令。  
   Conformity follows peers/majority; obedience follows authority.

5. **Groupthink vs Group Polarization**  
   Groupthink 是压制异议、过快共识；Polarization 是决策变得更极端。  
   Groupthink = suppress dissent / easy consensus; Polarization = more extreme decisions.
